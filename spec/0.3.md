# MLUE Specification — Phase 0.3

## 1. Overview & Objective
MLUE Phase 0.3 generalizes boundary constraints from **Entity-to-Environment** (outer walls) to **Entity-to-Entity** relational constraints in 2D space.

The goal of Phase 0.3 is to establish:
1. **Declarative Physical Boundaries**: An entity property `"solid": true` declaring that an entity occupies exclusive physical volume.
2. **Deterministic Pairwise Collision Geometry**: Closed-form mathematical formulas for detecting overlaps and resolving surface contact normals $\hat{n}$ between Circles and Boxes.
3. **Relative Velocity Reflection**:
   $$\vec{v}_{\text{rel}}' = \vec{v}_{\text{rel}} - 2(\vec{v}_{\text{rel}} \cdot \hat{n})\hat{n}$$
4. **Positional Separation**: Enforcing exact non-penetration clamping so entities do not stick or tunnel.

## 3. Pairwise Spatial Collision Mathematics

For two solid entities $E_1$ and $E_2$ in an environment of dimensions $W \times H$:

### 3.1 Circle-to-Box Intersection & Normal Resolution
* Let Circle have center $C = (c_x, c_y)$ and normalized horizontal/vertical radii $r_x = r \cdot \frac{\min(W, H)}{W}$, $r_y = r \cdot \frac{\min(W, H)}{H}$.
* Let Box have center $B = (b_x, b_y)$ and half-extents $h_w = \frac{\text{width}}{2}$, $h_h = \frac{\text{height}}{2}$.
* Closest point $P = (p_x, p_y)$ on Box to Circle center $C$:
  $$p_x = \text{clamp}(c_x, b_x - h_w, b_x + h_w)$$
  $$p_y = \text{clamp}(c_y, b_y - h_h, b_y + h_h)$$
* Offset vector in normalized units scaled by radius:
  $$\Delta x = \frac{c_x - p_x}{r_x}, \quad \Delta y = \frac{c_y - p_y}{r_y}$$
* Intersection occurs if:
  $$\Delta x^2 + \Delta y^2 < 1.0$$
* Surface Normal $\hat{n}$:
  * If $C$ is outside the box: $\hat{n} = \frac{(c_x - p_x, c_y - p_y)}{|C - P|}$.
  * If $C$ is inside the box: $\hat{n}$ is the unit normal of the closest box edge.
* Relative Velocity & Reflection:
  $$\vec{v}_{\text{rel}} = \vec{v}_{\text{circle}} - \vec{v}_{\text{box}}$$
  If $\vec{v}_{\text{rel}} \cdot \hat{n} < 0$ (moving toward each other):
  $$\vec{v}_{\text{circle}}' = \vec{v}_{\text{circle}} - 2(\vec{v}_{\text{rel}} \cdot \hat{n})\hat{n}$$

### 3.2 Circle-to-Circle Intersection
* Distance vector: $\vec{d} = C_1 - C_2$.
* If $|\vec{d}| < r_{x1} + r_{x2}$:
  * Normal: $\hat{n} = \frac{\vec{d}}{|\vec{d}|}$.
  * Reflect relative velocities along $\hat{n}$.

### 3.3 Box-to-Box Intersection (AABB)
* Overlap on X: $\Delta x = (h_{w1} + h_{w2}) - |b_{x1} - b_{x2}|$.
* Overlap on Y: $\Delta y = (h_{h1} + h_{h2}) - |b_{y1} - b_{y2}|$.
* If $\Delta x > 0$ and $\Delta y > 0$:
  * Intersection occurs. Contact normal $\hat{n}$ aligns with the axis of minimum penetration.
