## 1. Executive Overview

Standard floating-point arithmetic (IEEE 754) is non-associative: $(a + b) + c \neq a + (b + c)$. Furthermore, differing hardware architectures (x86 SSE/AVX with Fused Multiply-Add vs. ARM NEON vs. WebAssembly) apply subtle differences in intermediate rounding, leading to simulation trajectory divergence (desync) over extended timelines.

MLUE 1.4 introduces a **64-bit Signed Q32.32 Fixed-Point Arithmetic Core**, guaranteeing $100\%$ bit-exact simulation reproducibility across every CPU architecture on Earth.

---

## 2. Q32.32 Fixed-Point Representation

A real continuous number $x \in \mathbb{R}$ is encoded as a signed 64-bit two's-complement integer $X \in \mathbb{Z}$:

$$X = \lfloor x \cdot 2^{32} + 0.5 \rfloor$$

* **Scaling Factor**: $S = 2^{32} = 4,294,967,296$
* **Fractional Precision**: $\Delta_{\min} = 2^{-32} \approx 2.3283064 \times 10^{-10}$
* **Dynamic Range**: $[-2,147,483,648.0, +2,147,483,647.9999999997]$

---

## 3. Fixed-Point Arithmetic Operations

### 3.1 Addition & Subtraction
Fixed-point addition and subtraction are exact linear integer operations:
$$A_{\text{raw}} + B_{\text{raw}} = (a \cdot S) + (b \cdot S) = (a + b) \cdot S$$
$$A_{\text{raw}} - B_{\text{raw}} = (a \cdot S) - (b \cdot S) = (a - b) \cdot S$$

### 3.2 Multiplication
Multiplying two Q32.32 integers produces a 64-bit fractional component that must be scaled down by $2^{32}$:
$$(A \cdot B)_{\text{raw}} = (A_{\text{raw}} \cdot B_{\text{raw}}) \gg 32$$

### 3.3 Division
Dividing two Q32.32 integers requires scaling the dividend by $2^{32}$ prior to integer division:
$$(A / B)_{\text{raw}} = (A_{\text{raw}} \ll 32) // B_{\text{raw}}$$

### 3.4 Square Root ($\sqrt{X}$)
Square root calculation operates on the scaled integer value:
$$\sqrt{A}_{\text{raw}} = \text{isqrt}(A_{\text{raw}} \ll 32)$$

## 4. Fixed-Point 2D Vector Math (`Vec2FP`)

Every continuous 2D position and velocity is represented as a pair of Q32.32 integers:
$$\mathbf{v} = (X, Y)$$

* **Dot Product**: $\mathbf{v}_1 \cdot \mathbf{v}_2 = \frac{X_1 X_2 + Y_1 Y_2}{2^{32}}$
* **Squared Length**: $\|\mathbf{v}\|^2 = \frac{X^2 + Y^2}{2^{32}}$
* **Length**: $\|\mathbf{v}\| = \text{isqrt}((X^2 + Y^2))$
* **Vector Reflection**: $\mathbf{v}' = \mathbf{v} - 2(\mathbf{v} \cdot \hat{\mathbf{n}})\hat{\mathbf{n}}$

## 5. Architectural Parity Invariant (Benchmark B12)

* **Theorem (Cross-Architecture Parity)**:
  Because two's-complement integer addition, shift, and integer multiplication are bit-exact and hardware-independent, a simulation stepped for $K$ ticks in Q32.32 arithmetic produces identical output on x86_64, ARM64, WASM, and RISC-V:
  $$\text{SHA256}_{x86\_64}(\text{sim}_K) \equiv \text{SHA256}_{ARM64}(\text{sim}_K) \equiv \text{SHA256}_{WASM}(\text{sim}_K)$$
